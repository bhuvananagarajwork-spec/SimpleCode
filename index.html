❤️ Get Well Soon, Ashok

Scroll slowly… this is just for you ↓

Come closer, my love ❤️

let the noise of the world soften.

You’ve been brave in ways no one sees,

now it’s time to rest inside my care.

Breathe slowly 💞

with every beat you hear.

That sound is me reminding you,

you are deeply, endlessly loved.

If healing asks for patience,

I will give you all of mine ❤️

If your strength feels far away,

I’ll stay — quietly — until it returns.

Get well soon, Ashok 💖

Always loved. Always held. Always yours ❤️
