🤍 Get Well Soon, Ashok

Scroll slowly ↓

Come here, love 🤍

rest your tired heart next to mine.

If I could take the ache from your body,

I would carry it quietly — without a sound.

Breathe slowly 🌸

you don’t need to be strong right now.

I’m right here 🤗

in the pauses between your breaths.

Through night 🌙

through dawn ☀️

until your strength feels fully on ✨

Get well soon, Ashok 😘

🤗 I’m holding you in every heartbeat — always.
